Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SplitMate — quick answers, no fluff.

GETTING STARTED

How do I log in? ▼
Use username admin and password 1234.
How do I create a new group? ▼
Click the + New Group button in the left sidebar. Enter a name for your group (e.g. "Road Trip", "Flatmates") and it will appear instantly on your Home dashboard.
Can I add multiple groups? ▼
Yes — SplitMate supports unlimited groups. Each group gets its own expense log, chore list, and visual donut chart, all accessible from the sidebar.

EXPENSES

How do I add an expense? ▼
Go to the Expenses page or open a group and click + Add Expense. Fill in the group, category, description, payer, and amount — then hit Save. It shows up instantly.
What categories are supported? ▼
Currently: Grocery, Bills, Transport, Food & Drinks, Entertainment, Rent, and Other. Each category has a unique colour and icon so your spending is easy to scan.
Can I filter expenses by group? ▼
Yes. On the Expenses page you'll find filter pills at the top — one for All and one for each of your groups. Click any pill to show only that group's transactions.

CHORES

How do I assign a chore to someone? ▼
Open the Chores page or a group detail and click + Add. Choose the group, enter a chore name, then select who it's assigned to from the dropdown — which is auto-populated from group members.
How do I mark a chore complete? ▼
Click the checkbox on the left of any chore row. It turns teal and the chore name gets a strikethrough to indicate it's done. Click again to revert it to pending.

CHARTS & DATA

What does the donut chart show? ▼
Each slice represents a spending category for that group, sized proportionally to its total. The centre shows the group's net total. It updates live as you add or remove expenses
