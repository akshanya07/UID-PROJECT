Our Solution

No More Confusion

Off WhatsApp

Shared Living
Perfect for hostels, PGs, and apartments — manage rent splits, grocery bills, and utility costs together.
Fair Splits

Features
Everything your group needs, nothing you don't.
Expense Tracking

Group Management

Category-wise spending shown as a live donut chart per group — see where money goes.
